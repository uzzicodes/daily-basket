# Daily Basket operations

Mode: Operate. Web manager/owner panels and Android packer/rider app.

THESIS: Give each person one clear job, with server-enforced authority and an audit trail. The owner confirmed manager cash settlement on 21 October 2026; packers/riders never settle store cash.

OWN-WORLD: Extend Everyday Market with deep teal navigation, warm white work surfaces, precise Poppins web/system native type, exact product photographs and an original 3D teal basket brand mark. No supplier artwork or supplier-facing customer name.

STORY: New orders reach manager and picker/packer together immediately, without incoming order or bill approval. The packer checks every exact product and quantity, chooses Order packed, assigns an active rider and chooses Send for delivery. Dispatch requires completed exact-pack checks and the saved active-rider assignment. Assigned riders choose Order delivered after handing groceries to the customer; packers cannot deliver, cancel or settle. Before dispatch only, the manager or owner can use Item unavailable or Cancel order to cancel the whole order with an explicit reason. Item unavailable selects an order product and records that reason; it does not change global stock, remove a line or change other orders. Only after Delivered may the manager or owner verify actual delivery and exact physically returned cash, attest to both and choose Payment received. Short or extra cash leaves payment open. Delivered and cash_confirmed remain separate. Customer tracking has three English/Urdu stages: Order confirmed → Order dispatched → Order delivered. Pending and Packing map to Confirmed; Out for Delivery/Dispatched maps to Dispatched; Delivered maps to Delivered. Cancelled is separate and does not show a completed timeline. AppProvider refreshes all locally saved active orders every 15 seconds while foregrounded and on resume, even outside the order detail. Refresh uses each saved private receipt authorization, coalesces requests by order ID and skips Delivered/Cancelled orders during periodic checks. Order detail retains manual refresh, updated time and visible errors. This is foreground polling, not background push or live rider tracking. The owner sees private revenue, costs and profit coverage.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Preserve role/name navigation, current queue and exact photo/quantity detail. Manager sees Item unavailable and Cancel order for pre-dispatch issues, with explicit whole-order consequence/reason; a Delivered order exposes the exact cash field, delivery/full-cash attestation and Payment received. Packer views expose Order packed, active-rider assignment and Send for delivery at the eligible stage. Assigned rider views expose Order delivered after dispatch. Customer tracking uses three clear EN/UR stages and foreground refresh. Phone detail retains Back to queue.

FORM: An extension of the approved Everyday Market world. Keep the familiar operational layout and current stock/checkout infrastructure. No replacement visual-world roll; the new 3D logo is explicitly requested. seed: none (incumbent extension).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

QUALITY BAR: No cross-role access to owner accounts/profit, no rider settlement, no guessed profit, no silent cash discrepancies or duplicate state transitions. Manager and owner use web; packers/riders use one separately identified Android staff app with individual roles. Native device verification remains separate from browser preview and signed APK build checks.
