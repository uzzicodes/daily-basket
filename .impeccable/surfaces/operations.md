# Daily Basket operations

Mode: Operate. Web manager/owner panels and Android packer/rider app.

THESIS: Give each person one clear job, with server-enforced authority and an audit trail. The owner confirmed manager cash settlement on 21 October 2026; packers/riders never settle store cash.

OWN-WORLD: Extend Everyday Market with deep teal navigation, warm white work surfaces, precise Poppins web/system native type, exact product photographs and an original 3D teal basket brand mark. No supplier artwork or supplier-facing customer name.

STORY: New orders reach the manager and picker/packer together immediately. Incoming order and bill approval are removed. The picker starts Pending orders and checks every exact product and quantity; dispatch requires completed packing and an assigned active rider, with no approval gate. The assigned rider reports Delivered after customer delivery. Only after that report may the manager or owner verify delivery and the exact cash physically returned, then close payment. Packers and riders cannot settle store cash. Delivered and cash_confirmed remain separate states; short or extra cash leaves the bill open. The owner sees revenue, costs and profit coverage.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Role/name navigation, a clear current queue and selected order with exact photo/quantity lines. Manager sees immediate new orders and rider assignment; an out-for-delivery order explains that final verification waits for Delivered. Only a reported Delivered order exposes the exact returned-cash field, delivery/receipt attestation and Verify delivery & close bill action. Owner opens a period-filtered overview; native staff views prioritize the next job and permitted state action.

FORM: An extension of the approved Everyday Market world. Keep the familiar operational layout and current stock/checkout infrastructure. No replacement visual-world roll; the new 3D logo is explicitly requested. seed: none (incumbent extension).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

QUALITY BAR: No cross-role access to owner accounts/profit, no rider settlement, no guessed profit, no silent cash discrepancies or duplicate state transitions. Manager and owner use web; packers/riders use one separately identified Android staff app with individual roles. Native device verification remains separate from browser preview and signed APK build checks.
